# 20261009 M0 指定供应商组共同报价比较规则及验收
# 20261009 M0 Buyer-Selected Supplier Group Comparison Rules and Acceptance

## 一、已确认的业务决策
## 1. Approved buyer decision

采购员在报价比较页面**主动勾选至少两家供应商**，系统只使用这些供应商都有有效报价的共同物料，计算相同物料范围、相同 RFQ 需求数量下的供应商总额。
The buyer **explicitly selects at least two suppliers**. A like-for-like total covers only RFQ lines where every selected supplier has a valid quote, using the same requested quantity for each supplier.

未选中的其他供应商不应让共同报价项减少；不默认把本次询价的全部供应商加入交集。
Unselected suppliers must not shrink the common item scope; the whole RFQ supplier pool is not automatically included.

## 二、逐项判定条件
## 2. Eligibility of each RFQ line

以稳定的 `RFQ Line ID` 识别询价行，不能仅凭重复的物料编码把不同询价行误认为相同项目；报价价格从当前有效的已确认轮次/版本取得。
Use stable `RFQ Line ID` as item identity; never merge separate RFQ lines just because their material codes match. Prices come from the currently effective confirmed quotation round/revision.

仅当选定组内每家供应商对此行存在有限数值的 RFQ 数量适用单价，该行才进入共同项目集合。
An RFQ line enters the shared scope only when every selected supplier has a finite numeric applicable unit price for its RFQ quantity.

漏报、明确未报价、不供货、尚未确认的报价、没有数量适用阶梯的报价，以及未分配给该供应商的物料均不计入共同项目；也不以 `0` 代替缺价。
Omissions, explicit no-quotes, cannot-supply, unconfirmed quotes, unmatched quantity tiers and items not assigned to the supplier are excluded, never imputed as `0`.

当前 M0 没有正式币种、含税/不含税、采购单位换算和跨币种汇率模块，因此假设同币种、同税口径、相同物料单位，仅供模拟验证。
M0 lacks production currency, tax-basis, purchase-unit conversion and FX normalization. For demonstration only, amounts assume the same currency, tax basis and comparable item unit.

## 三、金额计算
## 3. Amount calculations

**自身已报价范围总额**：各供应商独立统计自身有有效报价项目的 `适用单价 × RFQ 数量` 之和，同时显示有效报价项目数；不同供应商覆盖范围不一致时，不作为低价排名依据。
**Own quoted-scope total:** Sum `applicable unit price × RFQ quantity` for each supplier's valid quotes, with item counts; totals over different scopes must not be used for low-price ranking.

**指定组同范围总额**：先求采购员所选供应商的有效报价询价行交集；每家只对交集中的相同询价行按原 RFQ 数量求和。各家共同项数相同，金额才可以在该演示口径下横向比较。
**Selected-group like-for-like total:** First intersect the selected suppliers' valid RFQ lines. Sum each supplier's `applicable price × requested quantity` over that *identical* set of lines. The resulting totals share the same item count and can be compared under the M0 assumptions.

**逐项理论最低价组合**：对每一条物料，只从全部有有效报价的供应商中取最低适用单价，不要求由一家供应商承接全部物料。
**Per-line theoretical lowest combination:** Choose the lowest valid applicable price across all eligible suppliers for each RFQ line; this is a combination, not a total offered by a single supplier.

**最终选定组合**：按采购员逐条选择的供应商求和，非最低价需有原因；标记待复核的人工选择在复核前不计入最终选定金额。
**Final selected combination:** Sum per-line buyer awards, requiring reasons for non-lowest choices; review-pending manual awards stay excluded until reviewed.

上述四种口径分别展示，不能混用“总价”三个字掩盖范围不同。
Present these four metrics separately; do not use the unqualified phrase “total price” when the scopes differ.

## 四、界面与边界状态
## 4. UX and boundary cases

进入页面不预选供应商；显示“请至少勾选 2 家”。勾选第一家仍不计算共同项；选够两家才进行组内交集计算。
No suppliers are preselected. Show “Select at least two suppliers”; a single selection is insufficient, and the group intersection is calculated only when two or more are chosen.

界面提供每家供应商复选框和“清空比较组”，并显示当前组的共同物料项目数、部分 RFQ Line ID 供核对；勾选状态在本次 M0 页面切换与重新渲染期间保留。
The interface has per-supplier checkboxes, Clear Group, a common-item count and example RFQ Line IDs for audit. Selection survives M0 tab switches and rerenders within the same page session.

小于两家供应商，或选定组没有任何共同有效报价时，显示“不可比较”和 `—`，**不显示同范围报价为 ¥0.00**。
With fewer than two suppliers or no valid common RFQ lines, show “Not comparable” and `—`, **not a false ¥0.00 common-scope offer**.

比较组选择不是定标动作，不应改变“最终选择”列，也不应撤销采购员已记录的非最低价选择理由。
Comparison-group selection is not an award action; it must not change final supplier selection or erase buyer reasons.

如果新报价确认，系统重新根据同一比较组计算最新有效报价；如果供应商被移出本次询价，则不自动用其他供应商替代原选择。
New confirmed quotes recalculate the current group's applicable prices. If a supplier is removed from the RFQ, no other supplier is silently substituted into the comparison group.

M0 不持久化状态，刷新或关闭页面会丢失选择；正式版持久化和审计日志在后续版本实施。
M0 does not persist state: page reloads lose the group selection. Production persistence and audit logs are later milestones.

## 五、验证要求
## 5. Acceptance requirements

测试 A：A/B 两家报价物料 1 和 2，C 只报价物料 2；选 A+B 比物料 1 和 2，选 A+B+C 只比物料 2。
Test A: A/B quote item 1 and 2, C quotes only item 2; group A+B includes 1 and 2, while A+B+C includes only item 2.

测试 B：相同两家不同单位价格，严格按每个 RFQ 行自己的需求数量求金额，再核对各家总额。
Test B: Different suppliers' unit prices are multiplied by the same RFQ quantity per line, then totaled and checked.

测试 C：一个选定供应商漏报、明确未报价、不供货、无有效阶梯或未被分配时，相关行退出交集，但不影响其他行计算。
Test C: Missing, explicit no-quote, cannot-supply, inapplicable tier or absent assignment removes only the affected line from the common scope.

测试 D：仅选一家或无共同报价时显示不可比较；未选中供应商的报价不影响结果。
Test D: Fewer than two selected suppliers or zero shared lines show not-comparable, and unselected suppliers do not change results.

测试 E：100 条虚构物料、10 家虚构供应商、两轮报价确认后，真实 Chromium 通过勾选/取消比较组验证交集及总额。
Test E: After confirming two rounds of quotations for ten suppliers on 100 synthetic items, real Chromium clicks the comparison checkboxes and verifies scope and totals.

## 六、后续待确认
## 6. Later policy decisions

本次不增加整体包价、中标比例限制、最低起订额分摊、运输费、税额与多币种换算。这些属于正式询比价模型的后续规则。
This phase excludes package-level tenders, award-share constraints, minimum-order charges, freight, tax and FX normalization. They belong to a later production comparison model.

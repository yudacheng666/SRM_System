## 二、按风险优先级修复
## 2. Prioritized fixes

### P0 — 在再次提交采购员验收之前必须先修复
### P0 — Must fix before the next buyer acceptance attempt

**AUD-01：未回收报价仍可用虚构价格参与比价和总额。**
**AUD-01: Unreturned quotes may receive generated demo prices in comparisons and totals.**

依据：`quoteMetaFor()` 在该供应商不存在报价提交时生成价格；`renderQuoteAnalysis()` 和 `renderQuotes()` 直接使用此结果。其他供应商已有正式报价时，尚未提交的供应商仍可能参与有效最低价及总额计算。
Evidence: `quoteMetaFor()` synthesizes prices when a supplier has no submission; `renderQuoteAnalysis()` and `renderQuotes()` use these values. A supplier without returned quotes can still affect minima and totals when other suppliers have submitted actual data (L1240–1262).

修复要求：显式区分“纯演示样本模式”和“真实回收验证模式”；一旦按真实报价流程测试，未回收必须显示缺报价，不能自动生成价格；历史采购价如为模拟也应明确标记。
Required fix: Explicitly separate pure demo fixture mode from returned-quotation validation. In the latter, unreturned lines must remain missing instead of receiving synthetic prices; mark synthetic historical purchase prices clearly.

建议回归：有 A、B 两家供应商，仅 A 确认报价，B 无报价时，B 不得影响最低价、总额与默认选择。
Suggested regression: With suppliers A and B, only A's confirmed quote may affect award and totals when B has not quoted.

**AUD-02：编码重复时，按关键字段批量更新可能修改错误物料。**
**AUD-02: Duplicate keys can cause matched bulk updates to modify the wrong RFQ item.**

依据：`applyMatchedUpdates()` 使用 `items.find(...)` 选中首个命中；同一物料编码可保留多条 RFQ Line ID；当前没有重复键拒绝或人工选择，且直接改值。
Evidence: `applyMatchedUpdates()` uses first-match `items.find(...)`; equal item codes can legitimately occupy separate RFQ Line IDs, and no duplicate-key review or selection occurs before immediate mutation (L761–770).

修复要求：正式识别匹配 0/1/多条；只允许唯一命中直接更新；多候选暂停提交，显示全部候选和原因；先预览影响行，再确认写入。能修改数量的操作也必须运行完整数量校验。
Required fix: Distinguish zero, one and multiple matches; automatically update only unique hits. Ambiguous matches require preview and manual decision; preview affected rows before mutation and validate quantities.

建议回归：创建两个同编码不同 RFQ Line ID 行，导入以存货编码为匹配键的更新数据，不得自动只改第一条。
Suggested regression: Two distinct RFQ lines share a code; importing an update keyed by that code must not silently mutate the first line.

**AUD-03：历史确认后，分配关系及数量仍可被直接改动。**
**AUD-03: Quantity and assignment links remain mutable after confirmed quotation history.**

依据：批量字段更新直接写数量；供应商视角单选、反选、全部取消及按物料视角移除关系直接改 `assignments`；上述路径未统一检查该行是否已存在确认报价或其他不可逆业务记录。删除物料/整体移出供应商虽有保护，但不覆盖这些路径。
Evidence: Bulk changes can edit quantities and supplier-side click/invert/remove operations mutate `assignments` without a common confirmed-history guard. Item deletion and whole-supplier removal are protected, but these alternate paths are not (L753–770, L1131–1132, L1203–1215, compared with L772–780 and L1147–1158).

修复要求：统一历史保护函数，所有更改数量、关联、或删除业务对象的入口复用；已确认记录只允许创建新的业务修订/取消记录，不静默改变历史报价与询价范围。M0 没有正式“已发 RFQ”状态时应明确演示边界。
Required fix: Centralize a history guard and use it across quantity, relationship and deletion actions. Confirmed facts require an explicit revision/cancellation rather than silent changes; document M0's lack of a production issued-RFQ state.

建议回归：先确认一条供应商报价，再尝试跨视角解除该物料关联或批量改变数量，必须阻止或进入明确修订流程。
Suggested regression: Confirm a supplier quote, then attempt to unlink the quoted item or bulk-update its quantity; block the mutation or require explicit versioning.

### P1 — 业务准确性与导入体验，需要本轮修复
### P1 — Accuracy and import issues to fix in this iteration

**AUD-04：数据行少于表头列数一律报错，不符合允许缺少可选值的规则。**
**AUD-04: Rows with fewer cells than headers are always rejected, even when missing values are optional.**

依据：`parseHeaderTable()` 遇到 `cells.length!==headers.length` 均报错；对尾部缺失的非必需字段未补空值。
Evidence: `parseHeaderTable()` errors on every unequal column count and does not pad absent optional trailing fields (L582–611).

修复要求：数据行缺少可选尾部列时补空；缺主键/数量/单位等必填业务值仍报错；多出的数据列、无法安全对齐的情况报错，不猜错位。
Required fix: Pad missing optional trailing cells, but still reject absent keys and required transaction values; reject surplus or ambiguous cells without guessing alignment.

**AUD-05：首行同时混用中英文逗号时可能识别失败。**
**AUD-05: Mixed Chinese and ASCII commas in a header may fail parsing.**

依据：`parseHeaderTable()` 根据首行选定单一分隔符，并非同时支持两种逗号。
Evidence: `parseHeaderTable()` chooses exactly one delimiter for the whole text based on the first line rather than supporting both comma types (L583–595).

修复要求：按已确认粘贴规则兼容中英文逗号、Tab 和 CSV 引号，不拆字段内受引号保护的分隔符。
Required fix: Honor the confirmed Chinese/ASCII comma, Tab, and quoted-CSV behavior without splitting protected cell contents.

**AUD-06：提交报价时，错误行可能被直接忽略，用户未收到跳过明细的确认。**
**AUD-06: Quote commit can silently skip invalid rows without explicit skipped-row confirmation.**

依据：`commitQuoteImport` 过滤有效行后立即入库，随后清空预览；没有在提交前显示“提交 N 条、跳过 M 条”确认。
Evidence: `commitQuoteImport` filters accepted rows, immediately commits them, and clears the preview without confirming the number of rejected records (L1005).

修复要求：显示有效、待处理、跳过/拒绝数量及清单；明确部分提交规则，不能让采购员误以为整批已保存。
Required fix: Show valid, pending, skipped/rejected counts and rows; require explicit acknowledgement for partial commits.

**AUD-07：新报价确认会清空全部既有供应商选择及改选原因。**
**AUD-07: Confirming a new quotation resets all prior award choices and explanations.**

依据：`confirmQuoteRound` 无条件 `awards.clear(); awardReasons.clear(); setDefaultLowestAwards()`，即使只是对另一家供应商确认报价。
Evidence: `confirmQuoteRound` unconditionally clears `awards` and `awardReasons` and reselects minima, even for a different supplier's submission (L996).

修复要求：保留已明确选择的决策；新报价导致最低价变化时标记“需复核”，但不得悄悄删除手工选择原因；提供明确的重算动作。
Required fix: Preserve deliberate award decisions; flag records affected by new minima for buyer review rather than deleting award reasons, and offer an explicit recalculation action.

**AUD-08：一键沿用上轮对本轮全部缺失项批量生效，缺少逐项依据和确认信息。**
**AUD-08: Blanket carry-forward applies to all eligible omissions without per-item provenance or acknowledgement.**

依据：`carryForwardMissing` 遍历当前应报范围、自动取上轮有效记录并直接新增 `source:"carry"`；虽然点击按钮属于人工动作，但无逐项勾选或“供应商已确认维持原条件”的操作确认。
Evidence: `carryForwardMissing` loops over all eligible missing lines and writes `source:"carry"` immediately; a button click is intentional, but there is no item-specific selection or confirmation of the supplier’s unchanged terms (L1006).

修复要求：展示拟沿用项目、原轮次及原条件，支持选择并要求采购员确认来源依据；未确认部分继续漏报。
Required fix: Preview candidates with source round and terms, support selective carry-forward, require buyer acknowledgement of supplier confirmation, and keep unconfirmed items missing.

### P2 — 可在 P0/P1 后优化或验证
### P2 — Address after higher-priority issues, or validate

**AUD-09：大批量物料导入预览只显示前 50 行，后续冲突行不一定可见。**
**AUD-09: Large item previews render only the first 50 rows, hiding later conflicts.**

依据：`actions.slice(0,50)`；虽有冲突数量统计，但没有完整异常行查看入口。
Evidence: `actions.slice(0,50)` limits preview rows; counts exist, but a dedicated complete exception viewer is missing (L676–679).

修复要求：增加分页或“全部异常行”视图，不要求一次渲染全部物料。
Required fix: Add paginated preview or an all-exceptions view; avoid rendering the entire dataset at once.

**AUD-10：供应商视角“全选/反选/全部取消”实际直接增删业务关联，容易被误读为临时选择。**
**AUD-10: Supplier-side select/invert/remove actions directly mutate committed links and may be mistaken for temporary selections.**

依据：`sfLinkFiltered`、`sfInvertFiltered`、`sfUnlinkFiltered` 修改 `getSet`；与物料管理页的临时多选语义不同。
Evidence: Supplier-side bulk controls directly change `getSet`, unlike temporary selections on the item-management page (L1203–1215).

修复要求：保持“勾选即关联”的已确认设计，但按钮文案明确写为“批量关联/取消关联/反转关联”，高影响操作显示影响数及确认。
Required fix: Retain the confirmed direct-assignment model but label controls as link/unlink/invert assignments and confirm high-impact changes with affected counts.

**AUD-11：上次采购价为按行号计算的虚构金额，尚未连接实际订单数据。**
**AUD-11: Previous purchase prices are row-index-based synthetic amounts, not actual ERP order history.**

依据：`renderQuotes()` 使用 `95+((i*11)%50)` 生成展示金额；这属于 M0 演示，不能被报告或验收解读为历史采购记录。
Evidence: `renderQuotes()` computes the prior-price display from `95+((i*11)%50)`; it is an M0 placeholder, not imported purchase history (L1260–1262).

修复要求：对历史价格增加“演示值”标记；正式 ERP 历史成交价格导入留待 M1–M7。
Required fix: Label historical prices as synthetic; import real ERP purchase-price history in later milestones.

**AUD-12：独立的 100 物料 × 10 供应商、多轮报价导入测试文件仍需对当前表头做一致性验证。**
**AUD-12: The standalone 100-item × 10-supplier multi-round fixture still needs header and import compatibility verification.**

依据：源码内置 1,200 行、24 家供应商演示数据；本轮未核对外部测试工作簿，也未重新执行浏览器测试。
Evidence: The source seeds 1,200 items and 24 suppliers; no external test workbook inspection or browser test was performed in this source-audit pass (L442–457).

修复要求：优先复用现有合成演示数据，按实际 M0 表头补齐可重复用例；不把外部真实历史表上传到仓库。
Required fix: Reuse synthetic data and add repeatable tests with exact current M0 headers; never commit actual historical business spreadsheets.

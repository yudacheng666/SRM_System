## 二、“共同报价项”为什么容易是零
## 2. Why the old common-quote count often becomes zero

当前旧逻辑：对整个询价中的所有供应商，只有每一家都对相同物料有有效价格时，该物料才算共同报价项；任意一家不报价或未被分配该物料，该行即退出交集。
Old logic: A material counts as “common” only when every participating RFQ supplier has a valid price for it, even if a supplier was never assigned that material; any gap removes it from the intersection.

本项目允许“每家供应商只覆盖部分物料”的多对多询价，因此按全体供应商求交集，业务上不适合作为唯一总价比较指标。
This RFQ allows differing supplier coverages, so a single intersection across every supplier is not a useful universal total-price metric.

## 三、建议拆成三个不同的分析口径
## 3. Suggested separation into three comparison bases

**A. 自身已报价金额 / Own quoted-scope total：** 每家供应商仅累计其自身有效报价项目的金额，显示“X 项 / 总应报 Y 项 / 覆盖率 Z%”。金额不能直接跨供应商比较，因为物料范围不同。
**A. Own quoted-scope amount:** Sum each supplier's valid quotation lines and show item count and expected coverage. Do not compare raw totals across suppliers with different scopes.

**B. 指定供应商组共同报价 / Selected supplier group common scope：** 采购员先勾选 2 家及以上需要比较的供应商，仅对这些供应商均报出有效价格的物料计算同范围总额，并明确展示“共同项目 N 项 / 比较组 M 家供应商”；不能把未参与比较的供应商拖入交集。
**B. Selected-group common scope:** Buyer chooses at least two suppliers, and the shared-scope total includes only items with valid prices from every *selected* supplier. State the number of suppliers and common items.

**C. 全询价覆盖与最低价组合 / RFQ coverage and best-price combination：** 所有报价有效项进入逐物料对比，每项可在报过该项的供应商之间选定，计算理论最低金额和最终选定金额，并显示有多少项仍未报价或尚未选定。该口径不是单一供应商整体总价。
**C. RFQ coverage and per-material combination:** Compare each line among its actual quoting suppliers, calculate the theoretical low-price combination and buyer-selected total, and identify missing/unselected lines. Do not label this a total offered by any one supplier.

三种金额不应混称“共同报价”“总价”而不标注所涵盖的物料/供应商范围。
Do not call these three different metrics the same “total” without stating exact item/supplier coverage.

## 四、等待用户决定的业务边界
## 4. Pending buyer policy decision

**建议的默认方案：B 的比较组由采购员主动选择至少两家供应商；初次打开不自动用全体供应商求交集，避免误认为共同项为 0 就无法比价。**
**Recommended default: Require the buyer to pick at least two suppliers for B, rather than automatically intersecting the full RFQ supplier pool.**

候选其他口径：按某一物料集合或采购包指定比较范围；按“相同报价覆盖度分组”的供应商组；对未报价项目统一展示为缺失，不得填零或推测价格。
Other potential bases include a designated material/package scope or grouping suppliers by quote coverage; missing quote prices stay missing, never zero or estimated.

是否将 B 作为默认分析并修改原公式、是否纳入不同单位/币种/税额标准化，须得到用户确认并获得正式 ERP / 报价字段资料。
Whether to make B the default and amend the formula, and how to normalize units/currencies/tax treatment, requires user confirmation and official ERP/quotation field mapping.

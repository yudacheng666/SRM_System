# 20261009 M0 指定供应商组共同报价规则及浏览器验收
# 20261009 M0 Buyer-Selected Supplier Shared-Scope Rules and Browser Acceptance

## 1. 用户确认的口径
## 1. User-approved comparison scope

采购员**主动选择至少两家供应商**组成比价组，按选中供应商的共同有效报价物料集合计算同范围总价；不再使用全体 RFQ 供应商默认交集。
The buyer **explicitly selects at least two suppliers**. Totals are calculated only over items where every chosen supplier has a valid offer; the full RFQ supplier pool is no longer intersected automatically.

首次打开时比较组为空；只选零或一家时显示提示，不输出同范围金额。选够两家但无共同有效报价时提示“无可比项目”，不展示误导性的 ¥0.00 报价。
The group initially contains no suppliers. Fewer than two selections trigger guidance without monetary totals. A valid group with zero shared lines reports no comparable items rather than a misleading ¥0.00 quote.

## 2. 有效报价和同范围判断
## 2. Eligible offers and same-scope rule

以稳定的 `RFQ Line ID` 确认同一条询价需求，所有被选供应商对该行使用相同 RFQ 数量和物料单位，而非各家自报不同数量。
The stable `RFQ Line ID` identifies the same RFQ demand; all selected suppliers are evaluated at that line's common RFQ quantity and unit.

参与计算的价格来自 M0 比价函数 `quoteMetaFor`：在真实回收验证模式下，仅有效已确认供应商报价（包括适用数量档位的阶梯价）可参与。未报价、明确未报、不供货、无适用阶梯价或非有限价格都不能被自动补为零。
Prices come from `quoteMetaFor`: in quotation-return mode, only valid confirmed offers, including tiers applicable to the RFQ quantity, qualify. Missing, explicitly declined, cannot-supply, unmatched tiers and non-finite prices are never imputed as zero.

如果供应商明确有效报出了单价 0，M0 保留其数值；它与**没有报价**不是同一个状态。将来可另加零元报价例外审批，不在本次更改正常报价规则。
An explicitly valid quoted price of zero is distinct from **no quote**. Production controls may separately require approval for unusual zero-value offers; this change does not redefine accepted quotations.

各行金额按 `适用单价 × RFQ 数量` 得到，**逐行四舍五入至人民币分**，再分别汇总比较组各供应商金额；单位价格保留原有报价精度，不因显示金额是两位小数而截断单价。
Each line amount is computed from applicable unit price × RFQ quantity and **rounded to cents per line** before summation. Exported unit prices preserve original quoted precision rather than truncating to two decimal places.

当变更供应商勾选、报价确认状态或 RFQ 数量时，下一次计算使用当前有效记录；比价组不会更改报价原始凭证、供应商分配、历史报价或采购员最终选定结果。
Supplier selection and changed confirmed quote/scope data update the next calculation; the group does not mutate source evidence, supplier assignments, history or awarded supplier decisions.

## 3. 页面清楚分隔三类金额
## 3. Keep three amount meanings separate

**A 自身已报价范围金额：** 每家供应商自身有效报价金额合计，列出有效报价项目数。由于覆盖范围不同，该金额不能直接用来判断哪家报价低。
**A Own quoted-scope amount:** Sum each supplier's valid line amounts and show item count. Different scopes mean these values must not be ranked as if they covered the same items.

**B 指定供应商组同范围金额（本次默认比较方法）：** 采购员选中的两家及以上供应商共同有效报价的 RFQ 行，逐家计算完全相同的物料集合与数量，列出组内供应商、共同项目数、各家同范围总金额。
**B Buyer-selected group shared-scope totals (the approved default):** For two or more buyer-selected suppliers, calculate each supplier's amount on the **same** eligible RFQ line IDs and quantities; show supplier identity, shared line count and comparable total.

**C 逐物料理论最低价组合及采购员最终选择：** 面向 RFQ 全部已报价物料分别取最低价或已选定价格，统计覆盖物料数；属于多供应商组合，不代表任一家整单报价。
**C Per-line theoretical minimum and buyer-selected amounts:** Across the RFQ, show the cheapest valid offer per item and the chosen award total/count. This is a supplier combination, not one supplier's complete quotation.

B 的全部共同物料可导出 UTF-8 BOM CSV，包含 RFQ Line ID、物料编码、名称、RFQ 数量、单位，以及每家供应商对应的适用单价与行金额和同范围合计；提供前 40 行页面明细。
All B shared lines are downloadable as UTF-8 BOM CSV with IDs, item attributes, quantities, supplier unit prices, row amounts and comparable totals; the page previews the first 40 shared lines.

## 4. 具体计算例子（全部虚构）
## 4. Worked example (entirely synthetic)

| RFQ 行 | 数量 | 供应商 A | 供应商 B | 供应商 C |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 2 | 10 | 12 | 未报价 / No quote |
| B | 3 | 5 | 明确未报价 / Declined | 7 |
| C | 10 | 6（阶梯档）/ Tier | 7 | 6 |

选择 A+B：共同有效项目为 A、C；A 的同范围合计 = 2×10 + 10×6 = **80**；B 的同范围合计 = 2×12 + 10×7 = **94**。
Select A+B: shared items A and C. Supplier A totals **80**, and Supplier B totals **94** on exactly the same demand.

选择 A+B+C：仅 C 有三家共同有效报价；对应总额分别为 **60、70、60**；A 项和 B 项不能硬算。
Select A+B+C: only item C has all three offers, giving **60, 70 and 60** respectively. A and B are excluded rather than guessed.

上述价格不代表真实人民币采购成交价格；当前 M0 仍仅验证同币种和同税口径演示，正式币种、税率、计价单位转换以后续 ERP 字段映射为准。
These are not actual historical purchase prices. M0 assumes comparable currency/tax basis for demonstration; actual currencies, tax treatment and purchasing-unit conversion require later ERP field mapping.

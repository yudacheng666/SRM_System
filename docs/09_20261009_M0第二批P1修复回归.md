## 二、五项修复
## 2. Five fixes

### AUD-04 允许尾部可选列省略
### AUD-04 Allow omitted optional trailing cells

首行标题仍为必需；数据行少于标题列数时按列对齐并补空尾部可选值，主键、数量、单位等必需业务数据继续校验；多余数据列仍报错。
The first-row header remains mandatory. Shorter data rows are padded only at the trailing optional cells; required keys, quantities and units remain validated, while surplus columns are rejected.

### AUD-05 中英文逗号混合解析
### AUD-05 Mixed comma parsing

Tab 表格仍按 Tab 对齐；逗号模式允许中英文逗号混用，双引号内分隔符保留为字段内容，避免物料名称错位。
Tab-delimited tables continue to use tabs; comma mode supports Chinese and ASCII commas together, preserving quoted delimiters within values.

### AUD-06 报价部分提交需明确告知
### AUD-06 Explicit partial quotation commits

每次点击“写入当前报价轮次”均重新检查预览数据，汇总可提交数和拒绝数；存在错误行时必须确认“只提交有效行”，错误行保留在预览供修改，不能默默跳过。
Clicking Commit revalidates preview records and counts accepted/rejected lines. If any records are invalid, the buyer must acknowledge partial submission; rejected rows remain visible for correction.

报价身份重复、范围外、状态无效、价格非法或无适用阶梯价仍不得进入有效报价；修复不改变已确认版本不可覆盖原则。
Duplicate identity, out-of-scope, invalid status, invalid price and unmatched tier rows remain ineligible; confirmed revisions remain immutable.

### AUD-07 保留采购员人工定标
### AUD-07 Preserve buyer award decisions

确认新供应商报价时，保留已有人工选择及选择原因；对没有人工指定的物料才重新计算默认最低价。
Confirming another supplier's quote preserves manually chosen awards and their explanations; only non-manual defaults are recalculated.

新报价使人工选择不再最低价或失效时，保留选择、标记“需重新复核”，并在复核前不计入最终选择总额；采购员可重新确认或选择其他报价。
When a new quote makes a manual choice non-lowest or invalid, retain it with a Review Required indicator and exclude it from the final selected total until buyer review.

### AUD-08 沿用上轮逐项预览与确认
### AUD-08 Selective carry-forward preview and confirmation

“沿用上轮”不再一键批量写入所有漏报；先显示候选明细、原轮次/版本、适用价格、完整阶梯、交期及 MOQ，默认没有任何项目被勾选。
Carry-forward no longer copies all omissions immediately; show candidate lines with prior round/version, applicable price, tiers, lead time and MOQ, with nothing selected by default.

采购员必须勾选项目、填写供应商确认来源并勾选核实声明，再次确认后才写入；未选择的仍然漏报，原报价不供货、未知或当前数量无适用阶梯的项目不自动沿用。
Buyers must select lines, enter supplier-confirmation provenance, acknowledge their verification and confirm before recording carry-forward; unselected lines stay omitted, and ineligible historical conditions are never copied.

系统在沿用记录中保存原轮次、原版本和确认说明；切换轮次或报价范围改变时要求重新预览。
Records preserve origin round/revision and the buyer’s evidence note; switching quotation context or changing scope requires a fresh preview.

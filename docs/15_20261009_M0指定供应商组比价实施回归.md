## 二、已实现的业务规则
## 2. Implemented comparison rules

默认分析区域提供“采购员指定供应商组同范围比较”界面、供应商复选框和清空比较组按钮；进入页面不自动选中全部或任何供应商。
The default analysis area offers buyer-selected supplier-group comparison checkboxes and Clear Group; no supplier is preselected.

至少选择两家后，按 RFQ Line ID 查找组内每家供应商都有当前有效的、适用于该物料 RFQ 数量的单价。只有符合此条件的询价行进入共同报价范围。
After two or more selections, only RFQ lines with a currently effective applicable unit price from every selected supplier enter the common scope.

各供应商的组内同范围总额 = 对全部共同物料逐项执行“该供应商适用单价 × 该行 RFQ 数量”，再相加。组内每家参与比较的物料及数量完全一致。
The selected-group total for each supplier equals the sum of that supplier's applicable unit price multiplied by RFQ requested quantity over the identical shared item set.

明确未报价、不供货、漏报、未确认或数量无适用阶梯的报价不产生有效价格；缺价不计为零元、不得推测。
Explicit no-quotes, cannot-supply, omitted, unconfirmed and inapplicable-tier prices are excluded, never converted to a zero or guessed price.

不足两家或者共同项目为 0 时，说明“不可比较”，对应同范围总额显示 `—`，不显示 `¥0.00`。
When fewer than two suppliers are chosen or no shared quoted items exist, show “Not comparable” and `—`, not `¥0.00`.

自身有效报价金额仍按各家实际已报价范围独立统计，但明确提示“不同范围不可横比”；逐项理论最低价组合与最终选定组合独立显示。
Own quoted-scope totals remain separately calculated per supplier, explicitly labeled as not comparable across different scopes; per-line theoretical lowest and buyer-final selections are separate.

此比较组只影响分析区域，勾选比较供应商不改变最终定标选择，也不清除采购员已填写的非最低价原因。
Comparison-group selection affects only analysis; it does not change supplier awards or erase documented reasons for non-lowest choices.

在当前打开的 M0 页面内，切换页签、确认报价及重新计算时保留比较组选择；M0 不做刷新后的持久保存。
Selections persist across tabs and rerenders in the open M0 page; M0 does not persist selections across full reloads.
